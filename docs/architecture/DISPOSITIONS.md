# De-AI subsystem status and open-item dispositions

The disposition register for `sci-paper`, split out of
[`SCIPAPER_STANDARD.md`](../SCIPAPER_STANDARD.md) §11 on 2026-08-25.

This is a **record of decisions**, not independent policy. The standard remains
the single normative contract and nothing here can redefine a consequence class,
a measurement state, or a stopping rule; the evidence behind each row lives in
[`EVALUATION.md`](EVALUATION.md). The frontier's completion and the adoption
rule for any item are stated in the standard's §11.

---

| Item | Disposition | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Document-scale detection core (dispersion manifold, role coupling, split-conformal, per-stratum) | **Shipped, `measured`** | Calibrated on the complete human corpus; falsification and length-fair AUCs in EVALUATION [§9.2–9.5](evaluation/document-scale.md). |
| Salience hierarchy (`L2.salience_hierarchy`) | **Shipped, `measured`** | Per-bucket passage reference from the field's own banks; abstains where the reference cannot resolve above the gate (EVALUATION [§14](evaluation/narrative-salience-register.md)). |
| Domain register (`L0.register`) | **Shipped, `measured`** | Corpus document frequency with compound-by-rarest-part and macro-subscript handling; precision verified against native-term controls (EVALUATION [§14](evaluation/narrative-salience-register.md)). |
| Hypotaxis ratio as the formalisation of "flat prose" | **Rejected** | Refuted on the human abstract reference: the flagged manuscript sits above the human median in subordination, so flatness is not a subordination deficit (EVALUATION [§14.5](evaluation/narrative-salience-register.md)). |
| Thesis spine (§5.4) as a measured axis | **Shipped as a writing rule, deliberately unmeasured** | Three surface formalisations and then the replacement statistic were refuted, the last on its own pre-registered condition (EVALUATION [§9.6](evaluation/document-scale.md), [§14.5](evaluation/narrative-salience-register.md), [§15.1](evaluation/narrative-salience-register.md), [§15.2b](evaluation/narrative-salience-register.md)). No threshold, exit code, or advisory count may be built on it without new evidence. |
| Spine fraction as a discriminator | **Rejected** | Failed its pre-registered overturn condition; once the overturned verdicts were removed no domain-matched generated passage contained an unbound clause, and the residual separation was the cross-domain genre confound (EVALUATION [§15.2b](evaluation/narrative-salience-register.md)). |
| Inert-clause runs and inference-connective rate | **Rejected** | Inert runs do not separate once genre is matched, and the connective rate reverses sign between two AI banks, so a connective is not evidence of an inference (EVALUATION [§15.1](evaluation/narrative-salience-register.md)). |
| Subfield reference for the salience axis | **Rejected as unnecessary** | A weak-lensing top-tier bank reproduces the broad bank's gates and the manuscript's percentile; genre separates at the discipline level, not between astro-ph subfields (EVALUATION [§15.4](evaluation/narrative-salience-register.md)). |
| Subfield reference for the register axis | **Rejected as harmful** | A subfield bank is too small to express a rate at the register threshold, so core field terms flip to foreign on zero counts (EVALUATION [§15.5](evaluation/narrative-salience-register.md)). |
| Cooperative layer (`deai_partition`, `deai_anchoring`, `deai_provenance`, `deai_personal`) | **Shipped** | Partition/anchoring `measured`; provenance/personal `unmeasured` by design until the author supplies own history/papers. |
| `L3.voice` operating point | **Decided degraded** | Offline audit instrument; per-paragraph unit near-unjudgeable and document-level surprisal refuted (§2 L3, EVALUATION [§7](evaluation/learned-model.md), [§9.8](evaluation/document-scale.md)). |
| Field-topic-robust L3 operating point | **Not obtainable from this feature set** | Retrains across a 2.6× bank range and two profile rebuilds left the field-topic false-positive rate inside one retrain's own split range while headline AUC rose and held, so the confound is in the features, not the sample. Reopening requires a different feature set, not a larger bank (EVALUATION [§7.0a](evaluation/learned-model.md)). |
| Human-labelled validation for salience and register | **Closed on the repository side; the labelling itself is an author act** | The provenance half is measured on held-out refereed papers (EVALUATION [§17](evaluation/held-out-labels.md)); whether an individual advisory is good *advice*, and recall, only a labeller can say. Provenance labels are the chosen ground truth (2026-08), whether to hand-label is the author's decision taken outside this record, and the harness stands (`tools/label_findings.py`, whose docstring states its scheme). |
| `L1.distribution` operating point | **Decided degraded — refuted, not merely absent** | `deai_policy.json` is withdrawn as an adoption candidate. Measured one observation per document, burstiness reverses sign on adversarial prose and signposting runs below chance, the criterion that rejected the inference-connective rate; no threshold may be built on either statistic (EVALUATION [§16](evaluation/lexical-structure-uid.md)). |
| `L1.uid` operating point | **Decided degraded** | No field-policy-calibrated compatibility operating point; the surprisal path is measured not to add document-level power (EVALUATION [§9.8](evaluation/document-scale.md)). |
| Enriched surprisal features (roadmap rank 5) | **Done, not shipped** | Better than the three scalars but inert for the model-free detector, so recorded not shipped (EVALUATION [§9.8](evaluation/document-scale.md)). |
| Length normalization of manifold distance | **Rejected** | A length-confound exploit, not a noise correction (guardrail 9, EVALUATION [§9.8](evaluation/document-scale.md)). |
| Length-aware manifold (estimator-noise covariance) | **Rejected — built and refuted** | Its exact form was implemented and is length-*symmetric*: the short AI documents share the confound with the short human papers they are compared against, so the human out-of-fit rate is unchanged and two AI tiers move the wrong way (EVALUATION [§9.4c](evaluation/document-scale.md)). |
| Enlarging the conformal calibration split | **Rejected** | Tested because the achievable p-grid coarsens α = 0.05; moving documents from training to calibration leaves tail power flat and raises the human false-flag rate monotonically (EVALUATION [§9.4c](evaluation/document-scale.md)). |
| Baseline unification into one `(feature, unit)` object (rank 2) | **Done (v0.33.0)** | A second per-bucket axis would otherwise have carried a second copy of the quantile grid, percentile reader, sweep and calibration loop, and a percentile would have meant two things. `tools/deai_reference.py` now owns them and the `(feature, unit)` invariant, which caught the hedging axis calibrating at a unit with no lower tail (EVALUATION [§19.1](evaluation/discourse-and-citation.md)). |
| Jargon-conditional per-paragraph operating point (rank 3) | **Won't pursue as scoped** | The jargon confound is handled at document scale by per-stratum + conformal; a per-paragraph operating point is inconsistent with the L3-degraded decision. |
| `corpus_cos` ablation (rank 4) | **Won't pursue — cannot change a shipped decision** | An ablation runnable from the feature cache computes a different statistic from the recorded retrains, and its only consumer is a `degraded` audit-only classifier refuted on independent grounds, so no result could change shipped behaviour (EVALUATION [§18.7](evaluation/projection-and-operating-point.md)). |
| Citation placement as an axis | **Refuted (v0.33.0) — on its own pre-registered condition** | Two banks from a second model, differing in one prompt line, put the statistic on opposite sides of the human distribution; a statistic whose sign depends on which machine population was sampled is not a discriminator (EVALUATION [§20](evaluation/discourse-and-citation.md)). |
| Topic-orthogonal axes: cohesion, hedging (rank 6) | **Shipped (v0.33.0), one of them restricted** | Both flag the LOW tail, where the defect is absence. Cohesion ships at paragraph unit in all seven buckets; hedging has no paragraph-scale lower tail, so it runs at SECTION unit and only in `intro` and `method`, where a transfer test and a separation test agree (EVALUATION [§19.3–19.5](evaluation/discourse-and-citation.md)). |
| Long-form generation (`ai_long`) | **Standing falsification target — robustly measured** | A recorded measured limit: tail power stays at 0.000 across every metric, calibration split and seed tried while rank AUC shows the signal is present, so the operating point cannot reach it (EVALUATION [§9.7](evaluation/document-scale.md), [§9.4c](evaluation/document-scale.md)). |
| Single-seed tail-power figures as estimates | **Rejected as a reporting practice** | Per-seed spread on the manifold tiers is wider than several differences the record read as improvements, so §9 figures carry the spread and `tools/eval_docscale.py` reproduces them (EVALUATION [§9.4c](evaluation/document-scale.md)). |
| Hand labels as the only route to register/salience precision | **Superseded — half of it never needed a labeller** | A refereed paper's provenance is already a label for "does this axis fire on accepted prose", and held-out papers disjoint from the calibration banks now measure both axes (EVALUATION [§17](evaluation/held-out-labels.md)). `label_findings.py` keeps the remaining half: whether an individual advisory is good advice. |
| Comparing held-out against in-sample papers to estimate leakage | **Rejected — confounded** | The two populations are era-disjoint, so the contrast charges vocabulary drift to calibration leakage; a paired test on one population replaces it (EVALUATION [§17.3](evaluation/held-out-labels.md)), and its current reading is the last row of [§17.4](evaluation/held-out-labels.md)'s table. |
| Asymmetric projection between calibration and detection | **Fixed — it was 58.7% of the measured false positives** | The corpus df was built from body prose while the manuscript side read the whole raw file, so preamble and bibliography tokens read as foreign; `deai_register.body_only` restores the symmetry with no threshold changed, and `deai_salience` was checked clean (EVALUATION [§17.4](evaluation/held-out-labels.md)). |
| Count floor as the guard for a rate gate | **Rejected — replaced by a resolution guard** | A bank of n passages expresses no non-zero rate below 1/n, so a count floor cannot protect a rate gate; an unresolvable bank now reports `degraded` and its findings carry `measurement_status: degraded`, without being silenced (EVALUATION [§17.4](evaluation/held-out-labels.md)). |
| Asymmetric projection, second instance: citation commands | **Fixed — 46 command names, 8,835 leaking uses** | Unmatched citation forms fell through to argument substitution, so the bibliography key became a word: leaked surnames reached the register axis, and citation years entered the salience axis's numerals. Matched by shape rather than by an allowlist, because the tail is per-paper local macros (EVALUATION [§18.1–18.2](evaluation/projection-and-operating-point.md)). |
| Held-out bundles collectable as calibration input | **Fixed — a structural guard, not a caller convention** | `corpus_documents` filtered nothing, so the documented calibration command silently absorbed the whole held-out evaluation set; the guard now lives in the collector so every caller inherits it (EVALUATION [§18.3](evaluation/projection-and-operating-point.md)). |
| `L0.register` operating point | **Closed by refuting its premise** | Swept against held-out refereed papers and machine documents, rank AUC is below 0.5 at **every** setting, so no setting makes the axis a detector and the knob buys advisory volume. Cut at 15, the first point where a referee-grade paper is not flagged more often than not (EVALUATION [§18.4](evaluation/projection-and-operating-point.md); the current rate is the last row of [§17.4](evaluation/held-out-labels.md)'s table). |
| `L0.register` threshold as the vocabulary instrument | **Complemented by the zero-hit audit (v0.36.0); the 15-use rule stays as the selective instrument** | The 15-use floor misses every word a draft uses once; the audit lists every body word with corpus df 0, exemptions mechanical (standard §2 L0). It fires more on refereed papers than on machine text, so it ships as exhaustive advice with author dispositions, never as a detector (EVALUATION [§23.1](evaluation/vocabulary-and-residue.md)). |
| Asymmetric projection, third instance: headings | **Fixed — one regex owner** | Section headings sat in the manuscript's body projection but never in a corpus passage, so heading words read as zero-hit words and entered collocation pairs. `extract_sections.RE_HEADING_COMMAND` is now the single owner and `deai_reference.units`/`sections`, `deai_register.body_only` and `length_gate` all consume it (EVALUATION [§23.1](evaluation/vocabulary-and-residue.md)). |
| Collocation as an axis (`L2.collocation`) | **Shipped (v0.36.0), `measured`; glossary reading v0.38.0** | Asks whether a sentence joins words the field never joins, which the register axis cannot see; it caught the mentor-marked phrases on a manuscript under review and separates machine documents from held-out refereed papers on the document fraction (EVALUATION [§23.2](evaluation/vocabulary-and-residue.md)). |
| Residue axis (`L4.residue`) with a diff gate | **Shipped (v0.36.0), strengths set on refereed prose; absence rule added v0.37.0** | Strengths were measured on held-out refereed papers, not assumed, through four passes of projection fixes and demotions of procedure senses; the static negative-label rule stays ordinary because refereed papers carry it routinely, and the diff rule gates (EVALUATION [§23.4](evaluation/vocabulary-and-residue.md), [§23.4a](evaluation/vocabulary-and-residue.md)). The skill mirrors the family lists and the validator proves the mirror. |
| Structure families from the mentor's comments | **Shipped as auxiliary (v0.36.0)** | Paper-as-agent, wh-cleft and modifier-stack join the auxiliary class: named on the sentence, excluded from `template_score`, baseline fractions recorded per bucket (EVALUATION [§23.3](evaluation/vocabulary-and-residue.md)). |
| Condensation as a read-and-trim pass | **Replaced by the removal map (v0.36.0)** | `condense_map.py` lists every entry with the words it frees and `length_gate.py --require-shrink` turns the map's target into an exit code; a pass that cuts less than a refereed paper's own median target has not found what the map lists (EVALUATION [§23.5](evaluation/vocabulary-and-residue.md)). |
| Spaced units outside the unit vocabulary (rewrite fidelity, §8) | **Accepted limit** | A unit written with an ASCII space and absent from the vocabulary (`5 furlongs`) loses unit-level protection; its number stays a protected invariant, and `rewrite_reward` gates eligibility on it without emitting a finding. Recorded here so EVALUATION [§8](evaluation/learned-model.md) no longer carries it as an open item. |
| Enlarging `wgl-letter` to resolve the gate | **Rejected — format is not a domain** | Impractical, and wrong: the register axis measures domain vocabulary and a letter is a format, and on letter-format documents the letter bank flagged core field terms the field bank did not. A `<field>-<variant>` profile now judges against `<field>` and names the borrowed bank in every finding (EVALUATION [§18.5](evaluation/projection-and-operating-point.md)). |
